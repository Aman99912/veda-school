About Vedanta

A legacy of learning, a vision for tomorrow.

Since 1998, Vedanta International School has helped children discover who they are and who they can become. We pair a rigorous CBSE curriculum with inquiry-led teaching, so students learn to think deeply, speak confidently and act with kindness.

  • CBSE curriculum with global best practices
  • 1:18 teacher to student ratio
  • Smart classrooms and AI-ready labs
  • Value-based, inclusive culture
